Why MEIS

Not overwriting a field—remembering who owned the device, and when

In many peer systems, department and install location are flat “current” values. Change them and history disappears; past maintenance and costs become hard to attribute. MEIS stores ownership and location as time intervals, so repairs, maintenance, metrology, PM, and part replacements can be reconciled to the department in charge at that time.

Typical peer approach

Department / location = current fields only. Updates overwrite; historical ops and cost ownership drift.

Dept A → overwritten as Dept B (who was responsible? which cost center? unclear)

MEIS approach

Ownership periods · location periods with start/end. Ledger-linked ops stay attributable by interval.

Competitive edge

How MEIS differs from peer products

Compared with common hospital equipment-management products. “Typical peer” summarizes market patterns without naming vendors; the MEIS column reflects shipped capabilities and engineering conventions.

Dimension Typical peer approach MEIS advantage
Department / location Flat current fields; updates overwrite Time intervals; ops and cost reconcile to the right department by period
Editing timeline fields Silent edits of department / room common True change (close old + open new) vs correction—no quiet timeline edits
Historical backfill Hard to backfill, or still freely editable Draft → confirm; immutable after effective for clear audit boundaries
Ledger depth List + few tabs; ops scattered Complete fields + business sheets: repair / PM / metrology / loans on one asset
Document structure Headers without lines are common Master–detail required (plans, warehouse docs, ops executions)
Multi-channel Web / App capabilities diverge Shared features evolve together; channel is written and displayable
Audit & change Weak logs or missing before/after Entity change log in plain language; generation method for mixed sources
Archive capture Mostly local file upload Document-camera capture; copy / sort / preview engineered
Runtime sensing Ledger and monitoring often split Power monitoring linked to the asset; tags and readings multi-channel
Readability Raw codes and opaque FKs in lists Chinese / human labels for status; strong FKs as “code name”

Note: RFID / real-time locating remain on the roadmap and are not marketed as delivered here.
